Projects

Quantifying Convective Aerodynamic Heating During Ascent

ANSYS Fluent · Axisymmetric CFD · Mesh sensitivity study · AIAA Region V Conference

Built a 72,000-cell axisymmetric model to predict aerodynamic heating across five flight conditions from Mach 0.54 to 1.91, with boundary conditions derived from prior flight data. Peak stagnation-region heat flux reached 2.46 × 106 W/m². Comparing those results against the Sutton–Graves correlation showed it underpredicts by 183% to 452% in this regime — a gap wide enough to change which nose-tip material a collegiate rocket team should be selecting.
