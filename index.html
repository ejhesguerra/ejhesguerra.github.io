Quantifying Convective Aerodynamic Heating During Ascent

ANSYS Fluent · Axisymmetric CFD · Mesh sensitivity study · AIAA Region V Conference

Built an axisymmetric model to predict aerodynamic heating across five flight conditions from subsonic through supersonic, with boundary conditions derived from prior flight data.
