About Me

Hi, my name is Elvin, and I’m a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Missouri with interests in thermofluids, aerodynamics, simulation and analysis, and mechanical design.

I currently serve as Project Manager for the Mizzou Space Program (MSP), leading the design, manufacturing, and testing of our Argonia Cup rocket. We are competing against 30+ teams in a challenge centered on reaching a target apogee of either 7,000 or 14,000 ft while carrying golf-ball payloads and successfully executing airstarts.

During the summer of 2026, I worked at General Motors supporting high-volume manufacturing, where I led production teams, performed root-cause analysis on equipment and process failures, and developed improvements to reduce downtime.

What draws me to mechanical engineering is understanding how a system behaves, analyzing its performance, and using that insight to optimize and improve the design.
